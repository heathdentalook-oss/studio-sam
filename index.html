Three straight answers

Anything else? Call (416) 555-0148.

Placement uses local anaesthetic, the same freezing as a filling, so you feel pressure rather than pain. The guide keeps the opening small; most people need only ibuprofen for a day or two. Sedation is available.

Usually three to four months: one visit to scan and plan, about an hour for placement, 8 to 12 weeks of healing, then one visit for the crown. A bone graft adds three to four months.

A single implant with abutment and zirconia crown typically runs $4,200 to $5,600, including the 3D scan and guide. You get a written estimate after your consultation. Prices on this concept site are illustrative.
