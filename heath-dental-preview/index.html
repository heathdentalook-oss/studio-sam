Meet Dr. Heath.

Dr. Heath earned his DMD at Dalhousie University and is a member of the Nova Scotia Dental Association. He practises general, cosmetic and restorative dentistry for adults and families, from routine checkups to implants.

His approach is simple: explain first, then treat. You see what he sees on screen, hear your options and costs, and decide at your own pace. On weekends he is often out on the harbour.

Calm, by design.

Every visit is planned around time, comfort and clarity. Three things you will notice from your first appointment.

  • Unhurried appointments

    Checkups are booked at 60 minutes, so there is time to talk, ask and breathe. We run on time, too.

  • Comfort options

    Noise-cancelling headphones, warm blankets and nitrous oxide if you want it. Raise a hand and we pause.

  • Costs before treatment

    A written estimate before anything begins. We bill most insurers directly and accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP).

Good to know

What happens at a first visit?

About 60 minutes: a conversation about your goals, an exam, digital x-rays if needed and a cleaning when time allows. You leave with a clear plan and costs in writing.

Do you take CDCP and MSI for kids?

Yes. We bill the Canadian Dental Care Plan directly. Children under 15 with an MSI card are covered for exams, cleanings and many fillings through the Nova Scotia Children's Oral Health Program.

I'm nervous. How can you help?

Tell us when you book. We plan extra time, explain each step and agree on a stop signal. Headphones, blankets and nitrous oxide are always on offer.
